# Apero Portfolio Context

Last reviewed: 2026-10-18

## Purpose and audience

Portco Launch Control is a hiring portfolio project for Apero Advisors. Its primary audience is Benyamin Holley, Apero's Head of GTM. Michael Slawson is Apero's founder and President & CEO.

The user's goal is to earn a serious conversation about working with and learning from Apero. The project should make that case through inspectable work: clear business judgment, credible data modeling, disciplined implementation, safe activation boundaries, and an operating handoff another person could own. It should not rely on enthusiasm alone or present AI-generated volume as evidence of skill.

The employer research that motivated this project found no actionable public Apero opening inside its October 2026 recency window. This is therefore a targeted portfolio approach, not a response to a confirmed live job listing.

## Why Apero

Apero is relevant because its work combines account intelligence, CRM and RevOps infrastructure, signal routing, outbound systems, reporting, and code-based agents for complex B2B and private-equity environments. The project should demonstrate the same forward-deployed posture: understand an operator's commercial reality, make definitions explicit, build the system, expose failures, and leave behind something maintainable.

## Why Anchorpoint

Anchorpoint Fire & Safety is a fictional private-equity-backed operating company chosen to make the demonstration concrete. A regional fire and life-safety roll-up creates realistic problems involving acquired-company data, parent and facility identity, serviceability, recurring compliance work, branch coverage, CRM quality, market construction, and multi-channel execution.

The product remains an operating-company deployment. It is not a generic AI SDR, a fund-level portfolio dashboard, or a polished outbound demo detached from CRM and service-delivery constraints.

## What the work sample must prove

- The user can translate an ambiguous commercial problem into explicit operating definitions.
- CRM health is judged with deterministic, reviewable rules rather than opaque model output.
- Historical customer evidence informs ICP hypotheses without turning soft patterns into brittle qualification cliffs.
- TAM construction preserves parents, physical sites, contacts, provenance, confidence, and actual serviceability.
- Enrichment is bounded, progressive, and unable to bypass suppression or human approval.
- External activation is idempotent, auditable, reversible where possible, and deny-first when evidence is incomplete.
- The user can explain the data, rules, tradeoffs, failures, and customer-facing workflow without delegating the explanation back to the software.

## Narrative boundary

All Anchorpoint internal data and commercial outcomes are fictional. Public market facts may be real and sourced, but no synthetic record may be contacted or represented as a real customer result. The portfolio should favor ordinary, defensible assumptions over convenient success stories.
