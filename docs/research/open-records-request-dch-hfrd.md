Subject: Open Records Act request — licensed assisted living communities and personal care homes

To the Open Records Officer, Georgia Department of Community Health, Healthcare Facility Regulation Division:

Under the Georgia Open Records Act, O.C.G.A. § 50-18-70 et seq., I request a copy of the current list of all assisted living communities and personal care homes licensed by the Healthcare Facility Regulation Division.

For each facility, I request the following fields, to the extent they exist in your records:

- Facility name
- License or permit number
- Facility type, distinguishing assisted living communities from personal care homes
- License status and effective date
- Street address, city, county, and ZIP code
- Facility business telephone number
- Licensed bed capacity
- Name of the licensee or owning organization

I do not need personal information about residents, staff, or individual administrators.

If the records exist in an electronic format, I ask that you provide them as a spreadsheet or CSV file by email, as O.C.G.A. § 50-18-71 allows. If an existing standard report already contains most of these fields, that report is acceptable in place of a custom extract.

If fulfilling this request will involve any cost, please send me an estimate before proceeding.
